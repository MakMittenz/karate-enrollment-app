# Karate School Enrollment App

### UX/UI Design Case Study

A mobile enrollment app designed to help a local martial arts school transition toward an online enrollment system.

The app was designed to help parents plan ahead, streamline registration and payment, and create a more inclusive and user-friendly experience for a diverse community.

---

## Project Overview

**Project Duration:** August 2022 – December 2022

**My Role:** Lead UX Designer, UX Researcher, UX Writer

**Responsibilities:**

* User research
* Wireframing
* Low-fidelity prototyping
* High-fidelity prototyping

### The Problem

The challenge was creating an app that would be user-friendly for a diverse community while helping families with multiple children save time.

Key areas included:

* Making classes easier to browse
* Supporting multiple class selections
* Creating an easier checkout process
* Making the experience more inclusive and accessible

### The Goal

Create an inclusive enrollment experience with features that make the app easier and more user-friendly for all.

## Understanding the User

### User Research

I conducted an unmoderated usability study remotely within the United States.

* 6 participants
* Individual registration tasks
* Approximately 20-minute sessions
* Questionnaire completed after each session
* One participant was randomly selected to register a family of four

The research focused on making the app user-friendly for a diverse community and determining whether the experience could help families with multiple children save time.

![User Research Summary](Karate%20school%20case%20study4%20.pptx.png)

### Research Pain Points

The research identified several opportunities to improve the enrollment experience:

* Users wanted to browse classes without logging in.
* Users wanted to view and select multiple classes.
* Users wanted the ability to save favorite classes.
* Users wanted a faster checkout process with fewer steps.

![Research Pain Points](Karate%20school%20case%20study5%20.pptx.png)

### Persona

The persona helped represent user goals, frustrations, and needs throughout the design process.

## Starting the Design

### Paper Wireframes

I drafted screens on paper while keeping the research findings and user pain points in mind.

The initial concepts prioritized quick browsing and a streamlined ordering process.

![Paper Wireframes](Karate%20school%20case%20study8%20.pptx.png)

### Digital Wireframes

The digital wireframes focused on creating a clean experience while incorporating feedback from the research.

Users could search or navigate through the app without going through unnecessary screens.

A shortcut to favorite classes was also added to help expedite the checkout process.

![Digital Wireframes](Karate%20school%20case%20study9%20.pptx.png)

### Low-Fidelity Prototype

I connected the completed screens into a low-fidelity prototype.

The primary user flow focused on searching for and selecting a karate class.

## Usability Testing

I conducted an unmoderated usability study remotely within the United States with six participants.

The findings helped identify additional improvements that could make the experience smoother.

### Round 1 Findings

* Users wanted to save favorite classes.
* Users wanted larger font sizes.

### Round 2 Findings

* Users wanted a confirmation animation.
* Users wanted to browse without logging in.
* Users wanted faster checkout.
* Users wanted shortcuts.

![Usability Study Findings](Karate%20school%20case%20study11%20.pptx.png)

## Accessibility Considerations

Accessibility was an important part of refining the experience.

The design included:

* A hamburger menu for easier navigation
* Zoom functionality
* A speak-to-read option for users who have difficulty seeing text
* Familiar imagery and class images to help users identify classes more easily

![Accessibility Considerations](Karate%20school%20case%20study14%20.pptx.png)

## Takeaways

### Impact

The goal of the karate app was to provide a low-stress and personable enrollment environment for users of different ages and needs.

### What I Learned

During the design process, I learned that initial ideas need to be tested with users.

After the first usability study, I identified key improvements that could make the user flow more effective and inclusive.

The project reinforced the importance of using research and usability testing to guide design decisions.

![Takeaways](Karate%20school%20case%20study15%20.pptx.png)
